1. Purpose of this submission

I write on behalf of Mr A Client to submit an application for a Lawful Development Certificate for a Proposed use or development, under section 192 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (as amended by section 10 of the Planning and Compensation Act 1991), and the Town and Country Planning (Development Management Procedure) (England) Order 2015.
