# Critical Checks — Class D: porches

Statute: `source/GPDO-2015__Sch2-Part-1__Householder.md` line 585.
Guidance: p. 40.

## The right (D.)

*"The erection or construction of a porch outside any external door of a
dwellinghouse."*

Note **"any external door"** — not only the front door. A porch over a side or
rear door is equally within the Class.

Class D is the reason a modest front projection can be permitted development
even though A.1(e)(i) bars extending beyond the principal elevation. Class A and
Class D are separate grants; a porch is tested under D alone.

## Development not permitted (D.1)

| # | Check | Limit | Fails under |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | Not created by Part 3 change of use (G, M, MA, N, P, PA, Q) | → no right | D.1(a) |
| D-02 | **Ground area measured externally** | ≤ **3 m²** | **D.1(b)** |
| D-03 | Height above ground level | ≤ **3 m** at any part | **D.1(c)** |
| D-04 | Distance to any boundary of the curtilage **with a highway** | must **not** be within **2 m** | **D.1(d)** |
| D-05 | Not a Part 20 house | | D.1(e) |

## Points that decide real cases

- **D-02 is measured externally.** 3 m² is small — roughly 1.5 m × 2.0 m to the
  outside of the walls. Check the figured dimensions, and check whether the
  drawing gives internal or external sizes. If it is not stated, ask.
- **D-04 counts every highway boundary**, and `highway` in Part 1 **includes an
  unadopted street or a private way** (Part 1 Interpretation). A porch on a
  house set close to a private drive can fail this without anyone expecting it.
- There is **no article 2(3) land restriction** in Class D. A porch in a
  conservation area can be permitted development — but check for an article 4
  direction, and remember listed building consent is separate.
- **No conditions attach to Class D** — no materials condition, no glazing
  condition. Unlike Classes A, AA, B and C.
- If the structure exceeds any limit it is **not a porch under Class D**, and it
  is unlikely to be rescued by Class A because of A.1(e)(i). The answer is
  planning permission.

## Where the porch is not "outside an external door"

If the proposal encloses an area that does not sit outside an external door — a
storm lobby formed by infilling a recess, say, or a projection beside the door
rather than over it — it is not within the Class D description. Route it back
through `03__CLASS-ROUTER.md` as a Class A enlargement, where A.1(e) will
usually bite if it is on the front. Where the drawings are ambiguous about what
the structure encloses, **ask** rather than choosing the favourable reading.
