# Critical Checks — Class A: rear, side, wrap-around and two-storey extensions

Statute: `source/GPDO-2015__Sch2-Part-1__Householder.md` line 23 onwards.
Guidance: pp. 10–32 (diagrams on 10, 12–16, 18–28, 32).

**Read the statute for each item you rely on. This checklist tells you what to
check and where; it is not a substitute for the wording.**

---

**A-09 note.** Both Class A and Class E draw on the same 50% budget — count the
proposed extension, every existing outbuilding, garage, shed and the garden room
together. State the arithmetic: curtilage area, original house footprint,
existing buildings, proposed. If the drawings do not give the curtilage area,
**ask** — do not scale it.

**A-07 note.** This is the most commonly mis-stated fact in a PD appraisal. If
the planning history is not supplied, the answer is **CANNOT DETERMINE**, not an
assumption that the house is unextended.

## Part 2 — By extension type

### 2.1 Single-storey rear extension

| # | Check | Detached | Semi / terrace / other | Fails under |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-20 | Projection beyond the **rear wall of the ORIGINAL house** | ≤ **4 m** | ≤ **3 m** | A.1(f)(i) |
| A-21 | Overall height | ≤ **4 m** | ≤ **4 m** | A.1(f)(ii) |
| A-22 | Cumulative with an existing enlargement it joins | Total must still meet A.1(e)–(j) | same | **A.1(ja)** |

Measure from the **base of the original rear wall to the outer edge of the
extension wall**, excluding the eaves overhang — guidance p.18, diagram. Where
the rear wall is stepped or the house is L-shaped, see pp. 19–21.

### 2.2 Larger single-storey rear extension (the "neighbour consultation scheme")

Available only where A.1(f) is exceeded **and** A.1(g) permits it.

| # | Check | Detached | Other | Fails under |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-30 | **Not** on article 2(3) land | must not be | must not be | A.1(g) |
| A-31 | **Not** on a site of special scientific interest | must not be | must not be | A.1(g) |
| A-32 | Projection beyond the original rear wall | ≤ **8 m** | ≤ **6 m** | A.1(g)(i) |
| A-33 | Overall height | ≤ **4 m** | ≤ **4 m** | A.1(g)(ii) |
| A-34 | Single storey | must be | must be | A.1(g) |
| A-35 | **A.4 prior approval procedure completed before works begin** | required | required | **A.4** |

**A.4 procedure — what must be submitted** (A.4(2)):
- written description stating **(i)** how far it extends beyond the original
  rear wall, **(ii)** maximum height, **(iii)** eaves height — and **(iv)** the
  same three figures for the **total enlargement** where it joins an existing one;
- a plan indicating the site, showing the proposed development **and any existing
  enlargement it will join**;
- addresses of adjoining premises;
- developer's contact address, and email address if content to be contacted so;
- the fee.

**Timing (A.4(10)):** works must not begin before written notice that prior
approval is not required, or written prior approval, or **the expiry of 42 days**
from receipt of the information without a decision. Adjoining owners/occupiers
get **not less than 21 days** to make representations (A.4(5)(d)); public
holidays are disregarded (A.4(16)).

**Build obligation (A.4(11)):** as approved, or as notified where approval was
not required or the 42 days expired.

This is **PERMITTED DEVELOPMENT SUBJECT TO PRIOR APPROVAL** — never report it as
plain PD, and never as "needs planning permission".

### 2.3 Two-storey / multi-storey rear extension

| # | Check | Limit | Fails under |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-40 | Projection beyond the **original** rear wall | ≤ **3 m** | A.1(h)(i) |
| A-41 | Distance to the boundary **opposite the rear wall** | must **not** be within **7 m** | A.1(h)(ii) |
| A-42 | Not on article 2(3) land | more than single storey beyond a rear wall is excluded there | **A.2(c)** |
| A-43 | Roof pitch | must match the original house **so far as practicable** | **A.3(c)** |
| A-44 | Upper-floor side-elevation windows | obscure-glazed **and** non-opening below 1.7 m from floor | **A.3(b)** |

A-43 also applies where the new work **forms an upper storey on an existing
enlargement**.

### 2.4 Side extension

| # | Check | Limit | Fails under |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-50 | Height | ≤ **4 m** | A.1(j)(i) |
| A-51 | Storeys | **single storey only** | A.1(j)(ii) |
| A-52 | Width | ≤ **half the width of the ORIGINAL house** | A.1(j)(iii) |
| A-53 | Side elevation fronting a highway | not permitted beyond it at all | A.1(e)(ii) |
| A-54 | Article 2(3) land | side extensions **not permitted** | **A.2(b)** |

Half-width measurement and wrap-around cases: guidance pp. 22–28, diagrams on
every page in that range. **Measure against the original house, not the house
as extended.**

### 2.5 Wrap-around (side + rear)

Treat as **one enlargement** and apply **both** the rear limits (A.1(f)/(g)/(h))
and the side limits (A.1(j)) to the relevant parts, plus A.1(ja) cumulatively.

A wrap-around **cannot** use the A.1(g) larger-extension route for the side
element — A.1(g) permits only a *single storey rear* enlargement. If the side
element is what pushes the scheme beyond the limits, the answer is planning
permission. Guidance pp. 23–28.

### 2.6 Front extension

A.1(e)(i) prohibits extending beyond the principal elevation. A front extension
is therefore **not Class A development** in nearly every case. The exception
worth testing is a **porch**, which has its own Class D. See
`13__CHECKS__class-d-porches.md`.

## Part 3 — Article 2(3) land (A.2)

Conservation area, AONB / National Landscape, National Park, the Broads, World
Heritage Site.

| # | Check | Fails under |
|---|---|---|
| A-60 | No cladding in stone, artificial stone, pebble dash, render, timber, plastic or tiles — **any part of the exterior** | **A.2(a)** |
| A-61 | No extension beyond **any** side elevation of the original house | **A.2(b)** |
| A-62 | No more-than-single-storey extension beyond the rear wall | **A.2(c)** |
| A-63 | Cumulative total enlargement within A.2(b) and (c) | **A.2(d)** *(the 2019 guidance calls this A.2(ca) at p.30)* |

A-60 catches render. A rendered rear extension in a conservation area is **not**
permitted development, however modest.

## Part 4 — Conditions (A.3) — these bind the build, not just the consent

| # | Condition | Wording that matters | Cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-70 | Materials **of similar appearance** to the existing house exterior | **conservatory construction materials are excepted** | A.3(a) |
| A-71 | Any **upper-floor** window in a wall **or roof slope** forming a **side** elevation must be **obscure-glazed** | and | A.3(b)(i) |
| A-72 | …and **non-opening** unless the openable parts are **more than 1.7 m above the floor of that room** | measured from the floor, not from ground | A.3(b)(ii) |
| A-73 | Roof pitch to match the original **so far as practicable**, where more than single storey **or** forming an upper storey on an existing enlargement | | A.3(c) |

State these in every "PERMITTED DEVELOPMENT" verdict. A scheme that is PD on
paper stops being PD if built in the wrong material.

## Part 5 — What to report

For each check: the limit, the value shown **on the drawing**, the drawing and
revision it came from, and pass / fail / cannot determine.

Where the margin is **under 100 mm**, say so and recommend a Lawful Development
Certificate. Where a value was scaled rather than figured, say **scaled** and
put it on the information-required list.
