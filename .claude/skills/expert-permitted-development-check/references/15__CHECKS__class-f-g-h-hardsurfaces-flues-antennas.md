# Critical Checks — Classes F, G and H: hard surfaces, flues, antennas

Statute: `source/GPDO-2015__Sch2-Part-1__Householder.md` — F line 693,
G line 729, H line 765. Guidance: pp. 47–50.

---

## Class F — hard surfaces (driveways, patios, hardstanding)

### The right (F.)

Provision within the curtilage of a hard surface for any purpose incidental to
the enjoyment of the dwellinghouse, **or the replacement in whole or in part of
such a surface**.

### Development not permitted (F.1)

| # | Check | Fails under |
|---|---|---|
| F-01 | Not created by Part 3 change of use (G, M, MA, N, P, PA, Q) | F.1(a) |
| F-02 | Not a Part 20 house | F.1(b) |

Class F has **no** size limit, **no** height limit, and **no** article 2(3) land
restriction. It has one condition — and that condition is where schemes fail.

### Condition (F.2) — the drainage rule

Where **both**:
- **(a)** the hard surface is on land **between a wall forming the principal
  elevation and a highway**; and
- **(b)** the area covered, **or the area replaced**, exceeds **5 m²**,

then **either** the surface must be made of porous materials, **or** provision
must be made to direct run-off to a **permeable or porous area or surface within
the curtilage**.

| # | Check | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| F-10 | Is the surface in front of the principal elevation? | F.2(a) |
| F-11 | Does it (or the replaced area) exceed 5 m²? | F.2(b) |
| F-12 | If both: is it porous, **or** is run-off directed to a permeable area **within the curtilage**? | F.2 |

**Directing run-off to the highway or to a public sewer does not satisfy F.2** —
the receiving area must be within the curtilage. If the drawings show a
front driveway with no drainage strategy, that is a live issue: ask for it.

Guidance p.47.

### Related, but not Class F

- A **new or altered vehicular access onto a highway** is **Part 2 Class B**, and
  is barred by **art 3(6)** where the highway is a trunk or classified road, or
  where it would obstruct highway visibility so as to be likely to cause danger.
- **Dropped kerb works** need separate highway authority consent regardless.

## Class G — chimneys, flues and soil and vent pipes

### The right (G.)

Installation, alteration or replacement of a chimney, flue or soil and vent pipe
**on a dwellinghouse**.

### Development not permitted (G.1)

| # | Check | Limit | Fails under |
|---|---|---|---|
| G-01 | Not created by Part 3 change of use | | G.1(a) |
| G-02 | **Height above the roof** | Must **not exceed the highest part of the roof by 1 metre or more** | **G.1(b)** |
| G-03 | **Article 2(3) land siting** | Not on a wall or roof slope which **(i)** fronts a highway **and (ii)** forms **either** the principal elevation **or** a side elevation | **G.1(c)** |
| G-04 | Not a Part 20 house | | G.1(d) |

**G-02 wording is exact and awkward:** the bar is *"1 metre or more"*. A flue
exactly 1.000 m above the ridge **fails**. It must be **less than** 1 m. Do not
report "up to 1 m" — that is wrong.

**G-03 needs both limbs.** A flue on a highway-fronting rear elevation in a
conservation area is unaffected (a rear elevation is neither the principal nor a
side elevation). Read the drawing's orientation carefully.

Guidance p.48.

## Class H — microwave antenna (satellite dishes, aerials)

### The right (H.)

Installation, alteration or replacement of a microwave antenna on a
dwellinghouse **or within its curtilage**.

### Development not permitted (H.1)

| # | Check | Limit | Fails under |
|---|---|---|---|
| H-01 | Not created by Part 3 change of use | | H.1(a) |
| H-02 | **Number** | Not more than **2** antennas on the house or in its curtilage | H.1(b)(i) |
| H-03 | **Single antenna length** | Not exceeding **1 m** | H.1(b)(ii) |
| H-04 | **Two antennas — relevant size criteria** | Only **one** may exceed **0.6 m**; and that one must not exceed **1 m** | H.1(b)(iii) + **H.3(a)** |
| H-05 | **On a chimney — length** | Not exceeding **0.6 m** | H.1(b)(iv) |
| H-06 | **On a chimney — projection** | Must not protrude above the chimney | H.1(b)(v) |
| H-07 | **Cubic capacity** | Not exceeding **35 litres** | H.1(b)(vi) |
| H-08 | **Roof without a chimney** | Highest part of the antenna not higher than the highest part of the roof | H.1(c) |
| H-09 | **Roof with a chimney** | Not higher than the highest part of the chimney, **or 0.6 m measured from the highest part of the ridge tiles — whichever is the lower** | H.1(d) |
| H-10 | **Article 2(3) land** | Not on a chimney, wall or roof slope which **faces onto and is visible from** a highway; in the Broads, a waterway; not on a building over **15 m** high | H.1(e) |
| H-11 | Not a Part 20 house | | H.1(f) |

**H.3(b) — how length is measured:** in **any linear direction**, **excluding**
any projecting feed element, reinforcing rim, mounting or brackets.

### Conditions (H.2)

| # | Condition | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| H-20 | Sited, **so far as practicable**, to minimise its effect on the external appearance of the building | H.2(a) |
| H-21 | Removed **as soon as reasonably practicable** when no longer needed for reception or transmission | H.2(b) |

Guidance pp. 49–50.

## Cross-check before reporting

Classes G and H exist because A.1(k)(ii) and A.1(k)(iii) **remove** flues and
antennas from Class A, and B.1(e)(ii) / C.1(d)(i) remove flues from the roof
Classes. So a flue shown on an extension drawing is never covered by the
extension's Class A analysis — it needs its own G check, every time.
