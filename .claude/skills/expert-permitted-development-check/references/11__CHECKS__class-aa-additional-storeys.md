# Critical Checks — Class AA: enlargement by additional storeys

Statute: `source/GPDO-2015__Sch2-Part-1__Householder.md` line 237 onwards.

> ## ⚠ The MHCLG technical guidance does not cover this Class
>
> Class AA was inserted on **31 August 2020 at 9.00 a.m.** by S.I. 2020/755,
> art 3(2). The technical guidance was last updated **10 September 2019**.
> There is no guidance page, no diagram, and no worked example for Class AA.
>
> **Work only from the statute.** Do not borrow Class A or Class B reasoning by
> analogy — the tests are different and the procedure is different.

---

## The right (AA.)

The construction of:
- **up to two additional storeys** where the existing house has **two or more** storeys; or
- **one additional storey** where the existing house has **one** storey,

**immediately above the topmost storey**, together with any engineering
operations reasonably necessary for that construction.

**"Storey" (AA4(2)) excludes** any storey below ground level, and any
accommodation within the roof — whether original or a later addition. An
additional storey **includes** one that *replaces* roof accommodation.

---

## Part 1 — Eligibility (AA.1) — every one must pass

| # | Check | Test | Fails under |
|---|---|---|---|
| AA-01 | Not created by Part 3 change of use | Class **G, M, MA, N, O, P, PA or Q** → no right (**note: O as well, unlike Class A**) | AA.1(a) |
| AA-02 | **Not on article 2(3) land** | Conservation area, AONB, National Park, the Broads, WHS → **no right at all** | AA.1(b)(i) |
| AA-03 | **Not on a site of special scientific interest** | → no right | AA.1(b)(ii) |
| AA-04 | **Construction date window** | House must have been built **on or after 1 July 1948** and **on or before 28 October 2018** | **AA.1(c)** |
| AA-05 | Not already extended upward | No prior addition of storeys above the original house, **under Class AA or otherwise** | AA.1(d) |
| AA-06 | Overall height cap | Highest part of the roof after works ≤ **18 m** | AA.1(e) |
| AA-07 | Height increase cap | ≤ **3.5 m** where the existing house is one storey; ≤ **7 m** where more than one storey | AA.1(f) |
| AA-08 | **Neighbour height cap (non-detached only)** | Roof must not exceed, by more than **3.5 m**: *semi-detached* — the house it shares a party wall with; *terrace* — **every other building in the row** | **AA.1(g)** |
| AA-09 | Floor-to-ceiling of each additional storey | ≤ the **lower** of **3 m** or the floor-to-ceiling height of any storey of the **principal part** of the existing house — **measured internally** | AA.1(h) |
| AA-10 | Position | Additional storeys **only on the principal part** | AA.1(i) |
| AA-11 | No visible support structures | None on or attached to the exterior on completion | AA.1(j) |
| AA-12 | Engineering operations limited | Only works **within the curtilage** to strengthen **existing walls or existing foundations** | AA.1(k) |

**AA-04 is the usual killer.** A Victorian or Edwardian house is outside the
window. So is anything built after 28 October 2018. Ask for the build date; do
not infer it from appearance.

**AA-08 definitions (AA4(1))** — `detached` means it shares no party wall and has
no main wall adjoining another building's main wall. `terrace house` means it
sits in a row of **three or more buildings**. `principal part` means the main
part of the house **excluding any front, side or rear extension of a lower
height**, whether original or added.

## Part 2 — Conditions (AA.2(2))

| # | Condition | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| AA-20 | Materials of **similar appearance** to the existing house exterior | AA.2(2)(a) |
| AA-21 | **No window at all** in any wall or roof slope forming a **side elevation** — note this is an outright prohibition, not an obscure-glazing condition as in Class A | **AA.2(2)(b)** |
| AA-22 | Roof pitch of the **principal part** after the works must be **the same** as the existing — note "the same", not "so far as practicable" as in A.3(c) | **AA.2(2)(c)** |
| AA-23 | Use must remain **C3 dwellinghouse**, ancillary purposes excepted | AA.2(2)(d) |

## Part 3 — Prior approval (AA.2(3)) — mandatory, not conditional

Unlike the Class A larger-extension scheme, there is **no 42-day deemed
consent**. AA.3(13): *"The development must not begin before the receipt by the
applicant from the local planning authority of a written notice giving their
prior approval."* Silence is not approval.

### Prior approval is required as to (AA.2(3)(a)):

1. **Amenity of adjoining premises** — overlooking, privacy, loss of light;
2. **External appearance**, including the design and architectural features of
   the **principal elevation** and any **side elevation fronting a highway**;
3. **Air traffic and defence asset** impacts;
4. **Protected views** under the *Directions Relating to Protected Vistas*
   dated 15 March 2012.

### Also required before beginning (AA.2(3)(b)):

A **construction management report** setting out proposed hours of operation and
how noise, dust, vibration and traffic impacts on adjoining owners/occupiers
will be mitigated.

### The application must be accompanied by (AA.3(2)):

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Written description | Including details of any works proposed |
| Site plan | **Drawn to an identified scale**, **showing the direction of North**, indicating the site and the proposed development |
| Elevations | **Drawn to an identified scale**, showing **existing and proposed** elevations |
| Windows | **Position and dimensions** of the proposed windows |
| Fee | As required |

**This is a drawing-content requirement you can check directly.** A submission
without a north point, or without an identified scale, or without existing
elevations alongside proposed, does not meet AA.3(2) — say so.

### Post-consent conditions

| # | Requirement | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| AA-30 | Complete within **3 years** of the date prior approval is granted | AA.2(3)(c) |
| AA-31 | Notify the LPA of completion **as soon as reasonably practicable** | AA.2(3)(d) |
| AA-32 | Notification in **writing**, giving developer name, address of the house, date of completion | AA.2(3)(e) |
| AA-33 | Build **in accordance with the approved details** | AA.3(14) |

Consultation runs at **not less than 21 days** for adjoining owners/occupiers
(AA.3(5)(c)) and for consultees (AA.3(9)); public holidays are disregarded
(AA.3(10)).

---

## Part 4 — Knock-on effect

**Using Class AA extinguishes Class B.** B.1(h) — Class B is not available where
the house *"has been enlarged in reliance on the permission granted by Class
AA"*. Inserted by the same instrument, S.I. 2020/755, art 3(3)(b).

So a client who adds a storey under Class AA cannot afterwards add a dormer
under Class B. Say this in the appraisal — it is a real, and permanent, loss of
future rights.

---

## Part 5 — Reporting

Class AA schemes are **PERMITTED DEVELOPMENT SUBJECT TO PRIOR APPROVAL** at best.
Because there is no deemed consent and the approval reaches external appearance
and amenity in the round, the practical outcome is closer to a planning
application than to Class A. Set that expectation explicitly.
