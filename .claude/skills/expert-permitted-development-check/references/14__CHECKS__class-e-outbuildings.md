# Critical Checks — Class E: outbuildings, garden rooms, garages, pools, "annexes"

Statute: `source/GPDO-2015__Sch2-Part-1__Householder.md` line 619.
Guidance: pp. 41–46 (diagrams on 42, 43, 44, 45).

## The right (E.)

Provision **within the curtilage** of:
- **(a)** any building or enclosure, swimming or other pool **required for a
  purpose incidental to the enjoyment of the dwellinghouse as such**, or the
  maintenance, improvement or other alteration of such a building or enclosure; or
- **(b)** a container used for domestic heating purposes for the storage of oil
  or liquid petroleum gas.

Everything turns on **"incidental"**. See Part 3 below.

---

## Part 1 — Development not permitted (E.1)

| # | Check | Limit | Fails under |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-01 | Not created by Part 3 change of use (G, M, MA, N, P, PA, Q) | → no right | E.1(a) |
| E-02 | **50% curtilage ground-cover test** | Total ground area covered by **buildings, enclosures and containers** in the curtilage, other than the original house, ≤ 50% of the curtilage area **excluding the original house footprint** | **E.1(b)** |
| E-03 | **Not forward of the principal elevation** | No part on land forward of a wall forming the principal elevation of the **original** house | **E.1(c)** |
| E-04 | Single storey only | | E.1(d) |
| E-05 | **Height — dual-pitched roof** | ≤ **4 m** | E.1(e)(i) |
| E-06 | **Height — within 2 m of the curtilage boundary** | ≤ **2.5 m** | **E.1(e)(ii)** |
| E-07 | **Height — any other case** | ≤ **3 m** | E.1(e)(iii) |
| E-08 | **Eaves height** | ≤ **2.5 m** | **E.1(f)** |
| E-09 | **Not within the curtilage of a LISTED building** | Class E unavailable outright | **E.1(g)** |
| E-10 | No verandah, balcony or **raised platform** (>0.3 m) | | E.1(h) |
| E-11 | **Not a dwelling**, and not a microwave antenna | | **E.1(i)** |
| E-12 | Oil / LPG container capacity | ≤ **3,500 litres** | E.1(j) |
| E-13 | Not a Part 20 house | | E.1(k) |

**E-06 is the one that bites.** Most garden rooms sit near a boundary. Within
2 m of *any* curtilage boundary the cap is **2.5 m overall** — and that is
overall height, not eaves. A 2.6 m flat-roofed garden room 1.5 m off the fence
is not permitted development.

**E-08 applies regardless of roof form.** Eaves ≤ 2.5 m even where the 4 m
dual-pitch allowance is in play.

**E-02 shares its budget with Class A.** A.1(b) and E.1(b) are the same test
applied from two directions. Count the proposed extension *and* every
outbuilding together.

## Part 2 — Designated land

| # | Check | Where | Fails under |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-20 | **10 m² cap beyond 20 m from the house** — total ground area of buildings, enclosures, pools and containers situated **more than 20 m from any wall of the dwellinghouse** must not exceed 10 m² | **AONB · the Broads · National Park · World Heritage Site** (note: **not** conservation areas) | **E.2** |
| E-21 | **Nothing between a side wall and the boundary** — no part on land between a wall forming a **side elevation** of the house and the boundary of the curtilage | **article 2(3) land** (includes conservation areas) | **E.3** |

E.2 and E.3 have **different geographic scope**. E.2 omits conservation areas;
E.3 includes them. Check which designation actually applies before citing.

## Part 3 — "Incidental" — where annexes fail

E.4: *"purpose incidental to the enjoyment of the dwellinghouse as such"*
**includes** the keeping of poultry, bees, pet animals, birds or other livestock
for the domestic needs or personal enjoyment of the occupants.

That is an inclusive definition, not an exhaustive one. The working test:

| Likely incidental | Likely **not** incidental |
|---|---|
| Home office, gym, studio, workshop, hobby room | Anything providing **independent living accommodation** |
| Garage, store, shed, greenhouse | A unit with bedroom + kitchen + bathroom used as a separate home |
| Swimming pool, pool plant room | Accommodation let or occupied independently |
| Garden room for family use | A building that is, in substance, a **dwelling** — barred by **E.1(i)** |

A building containing sleeping accommodation is not automatically outside the
Class — a guest room used by the household can be incidental. A **self-contained
dwelling** never is: E.1(i) removes it expressly.

**When the drawings show a kitchen and a bathroom in an outbuilding, ask what
the intended use and occupation are.** Do not decide it from the room labels.
This is the single most common Class E dispute, and it is a question of fact and
degree that the drawings alone cannot settle. Guidance p.46.

## Part 4 — Related items often on the same drawing

| Element | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Decking / raised terrace** | Class E only if the platform height is **≤ 0.3 m**. Above that it is a "raised platform", barred by E.1(h) and by A.1(k)(i) — planning permission. Pt 1 Interpretation defines `raised`. Guidance p.29 |
| **Swimming pool** | Within Class E as "swimming or other pool" — the height limits apply to any enclosure or pool house |
| **Fences and walls around the outbuilding** | **Not** Class E — `building` in art 2 excludes gates, fences, walls and other means of enclosure in Schedule 2. Route to **Part 2 Class A** |
| **Hard standing around it** | **Class F** |
| **Air source heat pump beside it** | **Part 14 Class G** |
| **Solar on its roof** | **Part 14 Class A** — "a building situated within the curtilage of a dwellinghouse" |

## Part 5 — Evidence you will need

- Curtilage boundary and **area**, and the original house footprint area — for E-02.
- Distance from **every** part of the building to the **nearest** curtilage
  boundary — for E-06.
- Overall height **and** eaves height, with the roof form stated — E-05 to E-08.
- Distance from the nearest wall of the house, if the site is in an AONB,
  National Park, the Broads or a WHS — for E-20.
- Listed building status — for E-09.
- Intended use and occupation — for the incidental test.

If any of these is missing, the verdict is **CANNOT DETERMINE — INFORMATION
REQUIRED**, naming the item.
