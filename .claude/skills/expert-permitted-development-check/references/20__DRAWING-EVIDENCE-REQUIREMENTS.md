# Drawing Evidence Requirements — what a set must show to be appraisable

A permitted development appraisal is only as good as the drawings. This is the
list of what must be **figured on the drawing** before a finding can be made,
and the standard wording for asking when it is not.

## The governing principle

**A dimension is either figured on the drawing or it is an assumption.**

Scaling is not measuring. A scaled value may be used to *flag a risk* — "this
scales at roughly 3.1 m against a 3 m limit, so it needs confirming" — but it
can never support a finding of compliance. Say which you did, every time.

Where a figured dimension and a scaled position disagree, that is a drawing
error. Report it and ask; do not pick one.

## Minimum set for a Class A extension appraisal

| Drawing | Must show |
|---|---|
| **Site location plan** | Scale, north point, the site outline, and **every highway bounding or adjoining the site** — named, and identified as adopted or unadopted/private |
| **Existing block / site plan** | **Curtilage boundary**, curtilage **area**, original house footprint, **every existing building** in the curtilage with its footprint area, distances to boundaries |
| **Proposed block / site plan** | Same, plus the proposal, plus the **distance from the nearest part of the proposal to the nearest curtilage boundary** |
| **Existing floor plans** | The **original** house outline distinguished from later extensions, with dates if known |
| **Proposed floor plans** | Figured projection beyond the **original** rear wall; figured width of any side element against the **original** house width |
| **Existing elevations** | Existing ridge height, existing eaves height, ground levels |
| **Proposed elevations** | Proposed ridge height, proposed eaves height, **all four elevations**, window positions and which are obscure-glazed |
| **Section** | Through the extension, showing overall height and eaves height **from ground level at the base of the wall**, and floor-to-ceiling heights where Class AA is in play |
| **Roof plan** | Any alteration to the **existing** roof — rooflights, tie-ins, flues, solar |

## Additional, by Class

| Class | Also required |
|---|---|
| **AA** | Build date of the house; storey count; floor-to-ceiling heights **measured internally**; ridge heights of the **neighbouring** houses (semi) or **every house in the row** (terrace); scaled plan with north point; existing **and** proposed elevations; window positions and dimensions (**AA.3(2)** requires these expressly) |
| **B** | Cubic content calculation for the resulting roof space, measured **externally**; volume of **every previous** roof enlargement; the 0.2 m eaves set-back dimension |
| **C** | Protrusion of the rooflight beyond the **original** roof plane, measured perpendicular to the roof surface |
| **D** | External ground area of the porch; height; distance to **every** highway boundary |
| **E** | Overall height, eaves height, roof form, distance to the nearest boundary from **every** part, curtilage areas, **intended use and occupation** |
| **F** | Area of new/replaced hard surface in front of the principal elevation; drainage strategy and where run-off goes |
| **G** | Height of the flue terminal above the **highest part of the roof**; which elevation it is on; whether that elevation fronts a highway |
| **Part 14** | Protrusion dimensions; roof type; position relative to highways; MCS compliance statement for an ASHP; compressor unit volume including housing |

## Site and designation facts — not on the drawing, but needed

These come from the client, the LPA or a search — not from the drawings:

1. Conservation area / AONB / National Park / the Broads / WHS status
2. Listed building status (the house **and** anything in its curtilage)
3. SSSI
4. Article 4 directions affecting the site
5. Planning history: every consent, and **the conditions on them**
6. Build date of the original house, and dates of every extension
7. Whether the property is a house or a flat
8. Whether any existing works were carried out without consent

**If the drawings arrive without these, ask for them before appraising** — do not
appraise the drawings and caveat the designations at the end. Half of the limits
change with designation, so an appraisal written without them is not an
appraisal.

## Standard requests — use this wording

> **Information required — [drawing ref, revision]**
>
> 1. *Figured dimension* — [what], to establish [which check, citing the
>    sub-paragraph]. Currently [not shown / shown at a different value on
>    drawing X / scaled at approximately Y].
> 2. *Fact* — [what], to establish [which check].
>
> Until these are provided the element is **CANNOT DETERMINE**.

## When a drawing cannot be read

Say so plainly and specifically. Not "the drawing is unclear" but:

> Drawing 1042-P-03 Rev B: the figured dimension at the rear of the extension is
> illegible at the supplied resolution — it reads as either 3.0 m or 8.0 m.
> These fall on opposite sides of the A.1(f)/A.1(g) boundary and lead to
> different verdicts. Please confirm.

**Never resolve an illegible dimension by inference from the drawn geometry.**

## When drawings contradict each other

Report both, name both drawings and revisions, and ask which governs. Do not
average, reconcile, or assume the later revision is correct unless it is stated
to supersede.

## Revision control

Record the **drawing number and revision** of every drawing relied on in the
appraisal. An appraisal is valid only for the revisions named in it. Say that
in the output.
