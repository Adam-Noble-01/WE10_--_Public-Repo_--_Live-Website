# Critical Checks — Part 2 (minor operations) and Part 14 (renewables)

These sit **outside Part 1** but appear on most householder drawing sets.

# Part 2 — Minor operations

## Class A — gates, fences, walls and other means of enclosure

Statute: `source/GPDO-2015__Sch2-Part-2__Minor-operations.md` line 23.

| # | Check | Limit | Fails under |
|---|---|---|---|
| P2A-01 | Height **adjacent to a highway used by vehicular traffic** | ≤ **1 m** above ground level (2 m for a school, subject to a visibility proviso above 1 m) | **A.1(a)(ii)** |
| P2A-02 | Height **anywhere else** | ≤ **2 m** above ground level | **A.1(b)** |
| P2A-03 | Maintenance / improvement / alteration of an existing enclosure | Must not exceed **its former height**, or the height that would be appropriate under (a) or (b) — **whichever is the greater** | A.1(c) |
| P2A-04 | **Listed buildings** | No development **within the curtilage of**, or **to an enclosure surrounding**, a listed building | **A.1(d)** |

**P2A-01 says "adjacent to a highway used by vehicular traffic"** — a footpath
is not caught; a private way used by vehicles is. Check what the highway
actually is.

`building` in art 2 **excludes** gates, fences, walls and other means of
enclosure throughout Schedule 2 — so a boundary wall is never Class A or Class E
of Part 1, and never counts toward the 50% curtilage test.

## Class B — means of access to a highway

Permits an access **required in connection with development permitted by any
Class in Schedule 2** (other than Part 2 Class A), where the highway is **not a
trunk road or a classified road**. Cross-check **art 3(6)**.

## Class C — exterior painting

Painting the exterior of any building or work. Not permitted where the painting
is for advertisement, announcement or direction (C.1). "Painting" **includes any
application of colour** (C.2).

Note: in a **conservation area** painting is generally still permitted
development under this Class — but rendering or cladding a house is **not**
(that is A.2(a)), and an **article 4 direction** commonly removes Class C.

## Classes D and E — electric vehicle charging

Class D — an electrical **outlet** and its casing on a wall.
Class E — an electrical **upstand**.
Read the statute (lines 81 and 103) for the current dimensional limits; these
have been amended more than once.

# Part 14 — Renewable energy on domestic premises

## Class A — solar PV / solar thermal on a dwellinghouse

Statute: line 23; the **dwellinghouse** limits begin at **line 55** (A.1(2)).
The separate block-of-flats limits are at A.1(1) — do not mix them up.

| # | Check | Limit | Fails under |
|---|---|---|---|
| P14A-01 | **On a wall — protrusion** | ≤ **0.2 m** where the wall **abuts a highway**; ≤ **0.4 m** otherwise, measured from the perpendicular to the wall surface | A.1(2)(a) |
| P14A-02 | **On a balcony or roof enclosure — protrusion** | ≤ **0.2 m** where it abuts a highway; ≤ **0.4 m** otherwise | A.1(2)(b) |
| P14A-03 | **On a pitched roof** | ≤ **0.2 m** beyond the roof slope, **and** not higher than the highest part of the roof (**excluding any chimney**) | A.1(2)(c) |
| P14A-04 | **On a flat roof** | Not more than **0.6 m** higher than the highest part of the roof (excluding any chimney) | A.1(2)(d) |
| P14A-05 | **Conservation area or WHS** | Not on a wall, balcony or roof enclosure **which fronts a highway** | A.1(2)(e) |
| P14A-06 | **Scheduled monument** | Not permitted | A.1(2)(f) |
| P14A-07 | **Listed building** | Not permitted on the house **or on a building within its curtilage** | **A.1(2)(g)** |

Conditions (A.2): site so far as practicable to minimise the effect on the
external appearance of the building and on the amenity of the area; remove as
soon as reasonably practicable when no longer needed.

**Note the current text covers "plug-in solar" and balcony enclosures** — a
recent addition. Read A.1 in full rather than assuming the old roof-only rules.

Stand-alone (ground-mounted) solar is **Class B**, with its own limits — line 125.

## Class G — air source heat pumps

Statute: line 287. **Several of these limits changed recently.**

| # | Check | Limit | Fails under |
|---|---|---|---|
| P14G-01 | **MCS Planning Standards** | The pump **must comply** — this is a precondition, not a condition | **G.1** |
| P14G-02 | **Number — non-detached house** | Not more than **1** | G.2(a)(i)(aa) |
| P14G-03 | **Number — detached house** | Not more than **2** | G.2(a)(ii) |
| P14G-04 | No wind turbine on the same building or in the curtilage | | G.2(b), (c) |
| P14G-05 | **Volume of the outdoor compressor unit including any housing** | ≤ **1.5 m³** for a dwellinghouse (0.6 m³ for a block of flats) | **G.2(d)** |
| P14G-06 | **Not on a pitched roof** | | G.2(f) |
| P14G-07 | **On a flat roof** | Not within **1 m of the external edge** of that roof | G.2(g) |
| P14G-08 | Scheduled monument | Not permitted | G.2(h) |
| P14G-09 | **Listed building** | Not permitted on the building **or on land within the curtilage** | G.2(i) |
| P14G-10 | **Conservation area / WHS** | Not on a wall or roof **fronting a highway**; and not **nearer to a bounding highway than the nearest part of the house** | G.2(j) |
| P14G-11 | **Elsewhere** | Not on a **highway-fronting wall** above **ground floor storey** level | G.2(k) |

Conditions (G.3): not used **solely for cooling**; sited so far as practicable to
minimise its effect on the external appearance of the building and on the
amenity of the area; removed when no longer needed.

> **The old 1-metre-from-the-boundary rule is gone.** G.2(e) has been omitted
> from the current text. Any source still citing it is out of date. This is
> exactly why the statute must be read rather than recalled.

## Interaction with Part 1

- **C.1(d)(ii)** removes solar PV and solar thermal from Class C — solar on a
  roof is Part 14, never Part 1.
- Part 14 Class A covers **"a building situated within the curtilage of a
  dwellinghouse"**, so solar on a Class E garden room roof is Part 14 Class A.
- Part 2 Class A enclosures never count toward the Part 1 50% curtilage test,
  because art 2 `building` excludes them in Schedule 2.
