## General Statutory Obligations

#### Planning Consent vs Building Regulations Approval
- Regardless of whether Noble Architecture is retained for the technical phase, the Client remains legally obliged to ensure the project complies with the Building Act 1984 and the Building Regulations 2010 (as amended). "Planning Consent" (visual/townscape approval) and "Building Regulations Approval" (technical/safety compliance) are separate legal frameworks and one does not guarantee the other.


#### Planning Drawings are Concept Designs Only
- Planning drawings are concept designs and are not Building Regulations drawings, and they do not contain the level of technical detail required to demonstrate compliance (including, as relevant, structural connections, fire strategy, thermal continuity, ventilation strategy, and product performance evidence).

## Scope of Validity and Insurance Limitations

#### Fitness for Purpose Exclusion
- The Client acknowledges that drawings produced for Planning Permission are, by definition, not technically resolved for construction. Using Planning drawings for construction purposes is non-compliant and negligent.

## Dutyholder Roles and Responsibilities

#### Principal Designer Role Under Building Safety Act 2022
- The Client is advised that under the Building Safety Act 2022 and the Building Regulations etc. (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2023, statutory Dutyholder roles apply to ensure Building Regulations compliance, including the appointment of a "Principal Designer" for Building Regulations.


#### Phase-Specific Duty and End of Noble Architecture's Statutory Role
- Noble Architecture acts as Principal Designer strictly and solely for the duration of the appointed phases set out in the quotation and written appointment. Unless expressly retained in writing for the Building Regulations technical phase, Noble Architecture's appointment and statutory role as Principal Designer conclude upon submission of the Planning Application.
- From that point Noble Architecture ceases to be the designer in control of the design work, has no authority to manage or monitor technical compliance during construction, and holds no responsibility or liability for Building Regulations compliance during the construction phase.


#### Automatic Transfer to Contractor for Domestic Clients
- Under the amended Building Regulations, where a Domestic Client does not appoint a replacement Principal Designer in writing, the Principal Designer role transfers to the organisation in control of the building work (the Principal Contractor).
- By not instructing Noble Architecture for the Building Regulations technical phase, the Client acknowledges that the Contractor becomes the legally liable Dutyholder responsible for coordinating technical design, ensuring compliance with the Building Regulations, and managing competence and compliance evidence required for completion.


#### Compliance Declarations and Prohibition on Signing
- At completion, the Building Control Body may require signed compliance declarations and statements confirming Building Regulations compliance and supporting evidence records (sometimes described as the "golden thread" of compliance information).

- Noble Architecture will not and cannot sign compliance declarations for projects where Noble Architecture was not retained in writing for the Building Regulations technical phase. It is the Contractor's responsibility, acting as the Dutyholder Principal Designer where applicable, to sign completion statements and provide supporting evidence.

- If the Contractor is unable or unwilling to sign due to lack of competence or insufficient evidence, the Building Control Body may refuse to issue the Final Completion Certificate. Noble Architecture accepts no liability for any refusal or delay in certification arising from such circumstances.

## Technical Design Requirements

#### Structural Design (Part A) and Ground Conditions
- Noble Architecture does not provide structural calculations. It is the strict responsibility of the Client or the Contractor to directly appoint and pay a qualified Structural Engineer to calculate all structural elements including (as applicable) steel beams, lintels, foundations, load-bearing walls, and roof structures.
- Noble Architecture may, if requested, obtain a quotation from a preferred structural engineering partner, but the contract for structural works remains directly between the Client and the Engineer. The Structural Engineer's drawings and calculations take precedence over Noble Architecture's architectural layouts in any instance of structural conflict.


#### Ground Conditions and Site Investigation
- Architectural drawings are typically based on surface-level surveys and available information; Noble Architecture accepts no liability for costs, delays, redesign, or additional works arising from ground conditions discovered on excavation (including clay heave, made ground, obstructions, drains, or undocumented services).
- It is the Contractor's responsibility to verify ground conditions and constraints via appropriate investigation (including trial pits where necessary) prior to committing to construction methods and costs.


#### Fire Safety Responsibilities (Approved Document B)
- Compliance with Approved Document B is mandatory. Where Noble Architecture is not retained for the Building Regulations technical phase, it is the sole responsibility of the Contractor to demonstrate to the Building Control Officer that the works meet statutory requirements regarding means of escape, smoke detection, compartmentation, and fire resistance of materials.
- Where the project involves a loft conversion, the Client is advised that creation of a new storey can trigger requirements for a protected stairway, fire-resisting doors, and associated measures, and the Contractor must ensure compliance.
- Noble Architecture notes that open-plan ground floor layouts may conflict with fire regulations for loft conversions; if a Building Control Body requires additional measures (including a fire suppression system such as misting) to justify an open-plan arrangement, the cost, design, and installation of such systems fall entirely to the Client and Contractor.


#### Energy, Ventilation, and Overheating (Parts L, F, and O)
- Where applicable, Building Regulations compliance may require specialist calculations and evidence. It is the responsibility of the Client or Contractor to appoint and pay any required specialist, including an accredited Energy Assessor to produce design stage SAP calculations and associated documentation demonstrating Part L compliance.
- Noble Architecture does not produce SAP calculations. If calculations fail, any upgrades required to the specification (including insulation thickness, glazing performance, thermal bridging strategy, or services efficiency) are at the Client's expense.
- The Contractor is responsible for ensuring Part F compliance through correct provision and installation of background ventilators and mechanical extract systems to satisfy the Building Control Body.
- For new builds and heavily glazed extensions, Part O overheating assessments may be required (simplified or dynamic modelling) as a separate specialist service and fee not included within Noble Architecture's standard scope unless expressly included in writing.


#### Drainage and Public Sewers (Part H)
- If works are within 3 metres of a public sewer, a build-over agreement with the relevant water authority may be legally required. This is a separate process to Building Regulations approval.
- Noble Architecture is not responsible for fees, applications, programme impacts, or delays caused by statutory undertakers. The Contractor must locate all manholes, drain runs, and invert levels as early as practicable.
- If drains conflict with proposed foundations or levels, any redesign or additional professional services required are chargeable, and any construction changes are the responsibility of the Client and Contractor.

## Contractor Design Portions and Coordination

#### Contractor Design Portions and Specialist Elements
- Noble Architecture provides general arrangement and planning-stage design intent unless otherwise agreed in writing. Specific technical elements are Contractor Design Portions and must be fully designed, measured, coordinated, and warranted for compliance by the Contractor and or their suppliers.
- This includes (as applicable) pre-fabricated roof trusses, beam and block floor systems, underfloor heating systems, staircases and joinery shop drawings, and specialist glazing or curtain walling. All manufacturers and specialist contractors must verify dimensions on site prior to fabrication and ordering.


#### Documentation Coordination and Cross-Checking Responsibility
- Where Noble Architecture is not retained for the Building Regulations technical phase, it is the Contractor's sole responsibility to coordinate and cross-check architectural intent against the Structural Engineer's design, specialist manufacturer specifications, and Building Control requirements prior to procurement and construction.
- Noble Architecture maintains relationships with technical architectural technologists and compliance partners and can facilitate a Full Plans submission or technical package subject to separate instruction, fees, and lead times.
