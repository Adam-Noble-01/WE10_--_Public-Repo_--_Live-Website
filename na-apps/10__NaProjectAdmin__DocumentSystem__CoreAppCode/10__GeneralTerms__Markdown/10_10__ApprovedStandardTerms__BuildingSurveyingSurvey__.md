## Survey Methodology & Introduction

#### Hybrid Approach
- **Noble Architecture** employs a "Hybrid" measured survey technique. This combines traditional hand-measurement tools (laser distometers and tapes) with modern digital capture methods, including Mobile LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) for internal spatial mapping and, where permitted, drone-based photogrammetry for external roof and site analysis.

___

#### Purpose
- The objective of this survey is to capture a rapid, high-volume dataset of the existing building to facilitate the creation of the **TrueVision** concept model. It is designed to capture the "macro" scale of the building rather than the "micro" detail required for fabrication.

## Preparing for Your Survey (Client Guide)

#### Introduction to Preparations
- To ensure the survey is conducted as efficiently as possible and to minimise the time **Noble Architecture** needs to spend disrupting your day, we kindly request that you prepare the property using the following checklist.
- **Note:** Conditions such as highly reflective surfaces can interfere with LiDAR sensors. Preparing these areas in advance significantly improves data quality.

___

#### Interior Preparations
- **Reflective Surfaces:** LiDAR scanners "bounce" off mirrors. If feasible, please remove wall-hung mirrors. For fixed items (bathroom mirrors, mirrored wardrobes), please cover them; taping up newspaper or using bin bags works perfectly.
- **Windows & Light:** Please open all curtains and blinds fully to expose the window reveals. Ensure all internal lights are switched on prior to our arrival.
- **Clear Sills:** Please clear window boards/sills of ornaments and photos to allow accurate measurement of the window openings.
- **Accessibility:** Ensure unrestricted movement through all rooms. All internal doors should be unlocked. We will need to wedge doors open during the scan to create a "flow" of data between rooms.
- **Pets:** For their safety and to prevent "ghosting" in the scan data, please secure dogs and cats in cages or keep them on leads/in a separate room whilst the interior scan is taking place.
- **Clutter:** Please avoid hanging coats on stair newel posts or banisters (these obscure critical structural details). Remove clothes from wall hooks where possible.
- **Loft Access:** Please ensure the loft hatch is accessible. If you have a specific ladder or access system, please have this ready or provide instructions on its use.
- **Services:** Please clear the area around your Electrical Consumer Unit (Fuse Board), Gas Meter, and Stop Cock so their locations can be logged.

___

#### External Preparations
- **Drainage:** Please locate all known manhole covers. Ensure they are free from plant pots or heavy debris. **Noble Architecture** will handle the lifting of covers if required on the day.
- **Vehicles:** If possible, please move vehicles off the driveway or park them as far from the building perimeter as possible to allow a clear line of sight for the external scanner.
- **Garden Areas:** Ensure patios and pathways are clear of movable furniture or heavy clutter where practical.

___

#### Logistics & Safety
- **Weather Dependency:** Please be aware that LiDAR equipment and Drones **cannot operate in rain or high winds**. The survey is strictly weather-permitting and may need to be rescheduled at short notice for equipment safety.
- **Hazards:** Please inform us upon arrival of any known hazards (e.g., loose floorboards, exposed wiring, aggressive neighbours).
- **Neighbours:** We recommend notifying neighbours in advance, especially if a drone is to be used, to avoid privacy concerns.
- **Keys & Security:** If you are not present, please provide alarm codes. Due to insurance constraints, **Noble Architecture** cannot leave keys in a "safe place" (e.g., under a mat); we must post them through the letterbox upon departure.
- **Defects List:** It is helpful if you can compile a brief list of known issues (e.g., damp patches, cracks, recent repairs) to ensure these are photographed during the visit.

## Survey Limitations & Liability (Legal Exclusions)

#### Accuracy Standard (RICS Band E)
- **Concept Accuracy:** The survey is conducted in accordance with the principles of **RICS Band E** (Measured Surveys of Land, Buildings and Utilities). This level of accuracy is specifically defined for "Feasibility, Concept Design, and Planning" purposes.
- **Not for Fabrication:** The data captured is intended to produce a tenderable design package. It is **not** suitable for the manufacture of bespoke joinery, stone, glass, or steelwork without on-site verification. As detailed in previous schedules, all specialist manufacturers must derive their own millimetre-perfect fabrication dimensions.

___

#### Accessibility & Obscuration
- **"Line of Sight" Only:** **Noble Architecture** can only survey what is physically accessible and visible.
- **Overgrowth & Blockages:** Areas that are inaccessible due to dense vegetation, clutter, locked rooms, or unsafe structures will be **omitted** from the drawings or shown as solid blocks/assumed outlines.
- **Exclusion of Liability:** **Noble Architecture** accepts no liability for the accuracy of areas that were inaccessible at the time of the survey. If important features are hidden behind vegetation or clutter, their omission from the design is not a breach of contract.

___

#### Hidden Defects & Utilities
- **Surface Only:** This is a non-intrusive Measured Building Survey, not a Structural Survey or Condition Report. It does not assess the condition of the building fabric (e.g., rot, damp, subsidence).
- **Utilities Exclusion:** As per previous terms, the mapping of below-ground drainage, hidden pipes, cables, and ducts is strictly **outside the scope**.
- **Arboriculture:** This survey does not constitute a BS5837 Tree Survey. Trees will be plotted approximately for visual context only.

___

#### Data Interpretation
- **Scanning Noise:** The Client acknowledges that LiDAR data contains "noise" and tolerances. The conversion from Point Cloud to CAD/3D Model involves professional interpretation. **Noble Architecture** prioritises the "best fit" for the overall scheme over granular precision of individual uneven surfaces (e.g., bowing plaster).

## Data Interpretation & Geometric Rationalisation

#### The "Best Fit" Philosophy
- The Client acknowledges that existing buildings, particularly older properties, are rarely geometrically perfect. Walls bow, floors sag, and angles are rarely exactly 90 degrees. To produce a workable, efficient 3D Concept Model, **Noble Architecture** applies a process of "Geometric Rationalisation."

___

#### Averaging & Straightening
- In translating the raw LiDAR scan data into a CAD model, we deliberately "straighten" and "average" elements to create a clean, usable design. This includes, but is not limited to:
- **Walls:** Existing walls that may lean or bow are modelled as vertical (plumb) and straight.
- **Floors:** Floor levels are averaged across a room or storey to smooth out localised dips or unevenness in floorboards/screed.
- **Stairs:** Staircases are modelled based on an average pitch (nose-to-nose line), ignoring variations in individual riser heights or tread depths caused by historical movement.
- **Roofs:** Roof slopes are modelled as flat planes based on the average pitch, omitting sagging rafters or undulations in the tiles.

___

#### RICS Band E Tolerances
- This approach is consistent with **RICS Band E** survey standards for Feasibility and Planning. The Client accepts that cumulative deviations of **2 to 4 inches (50mm to 100mm)** across the length of a building are standard and acceptable side-effects of this rationalisation process.

___

#### Efficiency vs. Granularity
- Modelling every unique bow, sag, and crack ("High-Fidelity Surveying") would exponentially increase drafting time and costs, offering no benefit to the Concept or Planning process. **Noble Architecture** prioritises speed and design clarity over granular topographical precision.

___

#### Critical Warning for Specialists
- Because the model represents an "Idealised" version of the building, it **must not** be used for ordering fitted items. A joiner manufacturing a staircase or a window supplier measuring an opening must verify the actual physical geometry (including out-of-plumb walls) on site, as the drawing will not reflect these specific imperfections.
