## Disbursements and Third-Party Fees

#### Council Submission Fees

Application fees are set by the Government. **Noble Architecture** will calculate the required fee and request this from the Client. Funds must be transferred to **Noble Architecture** and **cleared at least one (1) working day** prior to the intended submission date. These are treated as pass-through expenses.

*Please note: The following fee schedule is based on government planning data researched as of January 2026. These figures are provided for indicative purposes and remain subject to annual indexation or further government amendments.*

| Application Type | 2026 Fees |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Householder Planning Application** | £548 |
| **Change of Use** | £610 |
| **Full Application (Fewer than 10 dwellings)** | £610 per dwelling |
| **Non-Material Amendment** | £44 (Householder) / £298 (Other) |
| **Planning Portal Upload Service Charge** | £70.83 + VAT (£85.00) |

#### Planning Portal Upload Service Charge

When submitting your application online via the national Planning Portal, an administrative service charge applies. This fee is levied directly by the Planning Portal (the national submission platform) to cover the costs of operating their system and processing payments. It is not a council fee or a fee charged by **Noble Architecture**.

Currently, the service charge is **£70.83 + VAT (£85.00)** and applies to all planning applications where the base statutory planning fee exceeds £100. This charge is payable at the time of submission alongside the main council application fee.

#### Ordnance Survey (OS) Data

The LPA mandates the purchase of licensed OS mapping data (Site Location and Block Plans) for all applications.

The cost is variable based on geography and area (typically budgeted at **£50.00** for standard domestic projects as of March 2025).

These funds will be collected at the **start of the documentation phase** to allow early integration of the data into the drawing set.

## The Nature of UK Planning Policy (Risk Acknowledgement)

#### No Guarantee of Outcome

The Client acknowledges that the UK planning system is subjective and discretionary. Decisions are made on a case-by-case basis by individual Case Officers, their countersigning Supervisors, and in some instances, elected Planning Committees.


#### Subjectivity

Whilst **Noble Architecture** utilises extensive experience to guide design choices, the planning process is inherently arbitrary. Decisions can be influenced by the personal interpretation of policy by officers, political motivations, or local objections which are outside **Noble Architecture's** control.


#### Zero Certainty

Consequently, **Noble Architecture cannot and does not guarantee a successful outcome.** 100% success is impossible to warranty in the UK planning environment. The Client proceeds with the application at their own risk.

## Scope of Agency: Submission Only Service

#### Limited Agency Service

To minimise professional fees, **Noble Architecture** is engaged on a "Submission Only" basis for the planning phase, rather than a fully manages Agent service.


#### Noble Architecture's Role

**Noble Architecture** will prepare all necessary drawings, complete the relevant forms, package the application, and submit it to the Local Planning Authority (LPA).


#### Handover of Responsibility

Upon receipt of the "Validation Letter" (or email) from the LPA, which confirms the application is registered and valid:

- **Noble Architecture's** role as Agent formally ceases.
- **The Client immediately assumes the role of Agent.**
- The Client is solely responsible for all subsequent correspondence, negotiations, and site visits with the Planning Officer for the remainder of the application lifecycle.

## Revisions and Additional Charges

#### Minor Amendments (Included)

To ensure a smooth process, **Noble Architecture** produces high-quality plans exceeding industry standards. However, should the LPA request minor clerical changes, notation clarifications, or minor dimensional tweaks requiring **less than 2 hours** of drawing time (plus associated administration), these will be undertaken **free of charge**.


#### Material Redesigns (Chargeable)

Significant design changes required by the LPA, or requested by the Client after submission, are chargeable.


#### Cost Protection

No additional billable work will commence without a formal quotation and written instruction from the Client. There is no risk of unauthorised charges.


#### Redesign Fee Structure
- This is a tiered pricing structure based on the number of hours required to complete the redesign.

## Timelines and Determination

#### Statutory Dates

The Validation Letter will state a "Target Determination Date" (usually 8 weeks). The Client is advised that this date is **indicative only**.


#### Extensions of Time

LPAs frequently require "Extensions of Time" due to staffing shortages or backlog. The Council has the power to extend the determination period significantly. **Noble Architecture** has no power to expedite this process.

#### Reasonable Timescales
The Client acknowledges that any revisions, amendments, or additional work requested are subject to reasonable timescales. Noble Architecture undertakes multiple projects concurrently, and as such, revisions and updates will be scheduled in accordance with existing workload, availability, and project priorities. No guarantee is given as to immediate turnaround times unless expressly agreed in writing in advance.

## Appeals and Specialist Advocacy

#### Refusal Protocol

In the event of a refusal, **Noble Architecture** recommends engaging a specialist Planning Consultant or Town Planner to handle the appeal. These specialists carry specific authority and statistically higher success rates than architects in the appeals process.


#### Political/Third-Party Objections

**Noble Architecture** accepts no liability for refusals driven by "NIMBY" (Not In My Back Yard) objections, neighbour disputes, or internal council politics/nepotism. Whilst we design to mitigate valid material planning considerations, we cannot control unfair weight given to local objections by the LPA.
