Quick guide (Google Maps photogrammetry)
  1. Open Google Maps in another tab and set up the 3D view.
  2. Click Choose tab, pick the Maps tab, then Share.
  3. Draw the frame over the 3D view, clear of the search bar, buttons and the Google Maps logo. Centre frame keeps the image centre on the view centre.
  4. Pick On change (for example 20%) or Timed, then Start recording. The floating window opens; click back on the Maps tab within the start delay.
  5. Orbit the camera. With Wait until settled, each image is taken once the view stops moving and the tiles have loaded.
  6. Pause to reposition, Stop to finish. The zip downloads straight away.

The floating window is never in the images. The mouse pointer is, if it sits over the frame: orbit with Shift and the arrow keys, or with the Maps rotate buttons outside the frame, or raise Still for to about 2500 ms so the pointer fades first. Do not zoom the browser or resize the window during a run.
