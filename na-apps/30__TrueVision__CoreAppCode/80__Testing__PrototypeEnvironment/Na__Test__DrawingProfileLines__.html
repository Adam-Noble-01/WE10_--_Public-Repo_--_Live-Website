Drawing Profile Lines Harness

Exercises the REAL Na__DrawView__ProfileLines__ module against a synthetic model made of the shapes a parallel view actually loses - a sphere, a cylinder, a torus and a curved wall - with one box as a control. The left tile is the flat render the drawing views did before; the right tile is the same render with the silhouette pass composited over it. Localhost only; not part of the app.
