PWA icons are required for installability. The following files must be
created and placed in this folder:

  Na__CadAuditToolsApp__Icon__192x192.png   (192 x 192 pixels, PNG)
  Na__CadAuditToolsApp__Icon__512x512.png   (512 x 512 pixels, PNG)

These are referenced by:
  - Na__AppInstallability__Manifest.webmanifest  (icon array)
  - Na__App__.html  (apple-touch-icon / rel="icon" links)

Suggested design:
  - Noble Architecture 'NA' monogram or CAD grid mark
  - Dark background matching the editor theme (#1e293b)
  - High contrast iconography for visibility at small sizes

Generation options:
  - Use Photoshop / Illustrator and export at exact pixel dimensions
  - Use an online PWA icon generator (e.g. https://www.pwabuilder.com/imageGenerator)
  - Request a generated image from the Cursor AI image tool
